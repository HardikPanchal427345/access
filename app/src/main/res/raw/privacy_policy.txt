AdMint Finance Toolkit - Privacy Policy

Effective date: March 14, 2026

AdMint Finance Toolkit is a calculator app for everyday finance use cases.

1) Information we collect
- We do not collect or store your calculator inputs on our own servers.
- The app shows ads using Google AdMob, which may process device identifiers and usage data according to Google's policies.

2) Ads and consent
- We use Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) to request consent where required.
- You may be able to manage privacy choices from the app's "Ad Privacy Options" action if available in your region.

3) Data sharing
- We do not sell personal data.
- Third-party partners (such as Google) may process data to serve and measure ads under their own privacy terms.

4) Security
- Calculations are performed locally on-device.
- We continuously improve app stability to reduce crashes and unexpected behavior.

5) Contact
- For policy questions, use the contact channel listed in the Play Store listing.

By using the app, you agree to this policy.
