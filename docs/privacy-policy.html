AdMint Finance Toolkit

Privacy Policy

Effective date: March 14, 2026

AdMint Finance Toolkit is a finance calculator app. This policy explains what data is processed when you use the app.

1) Information we collect

  • We do not store your calculator inputs on our own servers.
  • The app displays ads via Google AdMob. Google and its partners may process device identifiers, diagnostics, and usage data according to Google's privacy terms.

2) Consent and privacy options

  • Where required by law, we request consent using Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP).
  • If available in your region, you can review ad privacy options inside the app.

3) Data sharing

  • We do not sell your personal data.
  • Advertising and analytics partners may process data under their own privacy policies.

4) Security and retention

  • Calculations are performed locally on-device.
  • We aim to keep the app stable and secure through regular updates.

5) Contact

For privacy requests or questions, use the contact details provided on the Play Store listing.

6) Updates to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes are effective when published at this URL.
