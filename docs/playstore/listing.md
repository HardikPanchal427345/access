## Full Description
AdMint Finance Toolkit is a premium dark-mode finance utility app designed for speed, clarity, and daily usefulness.

Whether you are splitting bills, checking discounts, planning EMI payments, or calculating GST, AdMint gives instant and accurate results in a clean modern interface.

### What you can do
- Tip + Split: calculate total bill and per-person share in seconds
- Discount Calculator: quickly see savings and final payable amount
- EMI Calculator: estimate monthly installments, total interest, and total payment
- GST Calculator: add GST or reverse-calculate included GST

### Why users love AdMint
- Beautiful modern dark UI with gradients and subtle glass effects
- Lightweight and fast calculations
- Clean user experience with stable input handling
- Consent-aware ads using Google standards

### Privacy and ad transparency
- Financial calculations run locally on your device
- Google AdMob ads are served with consent handling where required
- Privacy options are available in supported regions

Install AdMint Finance Toolkit and simplify your everyday finance decisions.
